Floating surface

Required

Menus, popovers, dialogs and command panels always use an opaque near-black fill, in both themes.

Do not replace this surface with a lighter theme token.

Standalone icons

Never frame a lone icon with its own permanent outline or container. Let it sit on the surface.

Hover or focus for feedback. Group related actions when needed.
