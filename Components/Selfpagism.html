Welcome to the archive.

Stylesheets is a collection of visual guides for people who want their websites to look and feel like something they made themselves.

This example uses a wallpaper behind a central page window, regular navigation, ordinary text, a few images, and the sort of details you might add over time. It is a component reference, not a required layout.

The stylesheet shelf

Two current guides, two different approaches. They are prompts for making a real project, not polished website templates.

  • Modernimal — quieter interface design, careful spacing, and custom UI patterns.
  • Selfpagism — personal pages, diaries, weird little archives, GIFs, and visual self-expression.
NOTE: The central window is the frame. The wallpaper and the user's own graphics give the page its identity. Replace this sample text and imagery with the actual person's interests and references.
