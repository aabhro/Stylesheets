Welcome after dark.

Stylesheets is a shelf of visual guides for making a little corner of the internet feel unmistakably yours.

This specimen pairs a full-screen animated wallpaper with a floating, rectangular page window. Oxblood title bars, gothic serif lettering, tiny web buttons, and muted bone text give it a dark Y2K feel without turning the whole thing into a neon interface.

The style shelf / after dark

Two guides, two different approaches. Treat them as starting points, not rules to copy without thought.

  • Modernimal — quieter interface design, careful spacing, and custom UI patterns.
  • Selfpagism — personal pages, diaries, weird little archives, GIFs, and visual self-expression.
DESIGN NOTE: Let the wallpaper stay visible around the window. Keep the surfaces flat and nearly black, the edges thin, and the decorations small. The mood comes from the GIF, typography, and old-web details, not glow effects.
